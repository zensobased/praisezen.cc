spotify connect

One-time step that lets the profile read your currently playing track straight from the Spotify Web API. Everything happens in this tab — no server, and no client secret.

  1. Create an app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard and copy its client ID.
  2. In that app’s settings, add this exact redirect URI:

loading…

scope requested: user-read-currently-playing

refresh token

Set SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID and SPOTIFY_REFRESH_TOKEN near the top of script.js. Treat the token like a password: anyone holding it can read your playback state.
